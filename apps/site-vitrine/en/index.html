How it works

One delivery, four hands, a single record

The merchant, dispatch, the driver and accounting work on the same order — each from their own screen, with their own permissions and language.

  1. 1Merchant

    The merchant books

    From their portal or their own system: recipient, location, parcel size, amount to collect, required level of proof.

  2. 2Dispatch

    Dispatch assigns

    Each order is offered to the least-loaded driver in its zone, who accepts or declines. The dispatcher stays in control of the exceptions.

  3. 3Driver

    The driver delivers

    Pickup, scan, delivery, proof, collection. Every step is time-stamped — even when the network drops.

  4. 4Accounting

    Accounting reconciles

    Driver float, branch deposit, explained discrepancies, merchant payout net of commission, invoice.

The driver app

A whole day that fits in a phone

Built for one hand, in full sun, with a network that drops. Your driver never has to guess what the app expects next.

  • Offline first

    Status, proof, collection: everything is queued and replayed when the network returns, never counted twice.

  • Proof, set per delivery

    None, photo, signature, or both. You decide what proof is worth paying for.

  • Collection at the door

    The amount to collect is on the delivery; each collection feeds the driver’s cash float, delivery by delivery.

  • The customer’s number stays private

    It is never sent to the driver’s phone.

  • Driving time tracked

    Nine hours a day at most, mandatory breaks, an alert before the limit.

Cash on delivery

Every cash collection, tracked to the merchant payout

This is where carriers lose money — and merchants. Portrail follows the cash collected at the door from end to end, and every discrepancy carries a reason.

  1. Collected at the doorBy the driver, time-stamped, even offline.
  2. Added to the floatDelivery by delivery, with a cap on cash in hand.
  3. Declared, then depositedAt the branch: expected, declared and deposited reconciled.
  4. Discrepancy explainedNo gap disappears without a reason.
  5. Paid out to the merchantNet of commission, itemised in their wallet.
  6. InvoicedMerchant invoices, disputes opened and followed up.

Fraud detection

A risk score built from signals: cash discrepancies, deliveries outside the zone, failures without proof, late deposits, undeclared collections. An alert calls for human review — never an automatic penalty.

Traceable refunds

Cash already collected on a cancelled or returned order is refunded and linked back to its order. Nothing leaves the till without a trace.

Location by link New

The customer shares a location. The driver finds the door.

Customers share their location on WhatsApp, as a link. Paste it into the order — or drop a pin on the map — and the driver opens the exact spot with one tap.

Security and data

Your data, your rules

A carrier entrusts us with its merchants, its customers and its cash. Portrail is built so that nothing leaks and everything can be traced.

  • An isolated space per company

    Each carrier has its own data space. No data crosses from one company to another.

  • Bounded roles

    Admin, dispatcher, accountant, merchant, driver: everyone sees and does only what their role allows, on screen and in the API.

  • An audit log

    Sensitive actions are recorded: who did what, and when.

  • Consent and personal data

    No SMS or WhatsApp without the recipient’s consent. Personal data can be exported and anonymised; home locations are never exposed on public tracking.

  • French and Arabic

    Bilingual interfaces, with a fully mirrored right-to-left layout in Arabic — not just a translation.

  • Automatic, and under control

    Once switched on, assignment spreads jobs fairly and drivers remain free to decline. Whatever it cannot place comes back to you with the reason. It flags risks without penalising anyone and blocks any message sent without consent.

FAQ

What people ask us

Another question? Write to us — we reply within two business days.

What size of company is Portrail built for?

From a handful of drivers to several dozen, in one city or many. Each client company gets its own data space, and roll-out happens city by city rather than all at once.

Does the driver app work without a network connection?

Yes. Status changes, proof of delivery and cash collection all work offline: they are queued and replayed when the network comes back, without ever applying the same action twice.

How is cash collected on delivery tracked?

End to end: collection at the customer’s door, the driver’s cash float updated delivery by delivery, the driver’s declaration, the deposit at the branch, any discrepancy explained, then the payout to the merchant net of commission.

Are orders assigned automatically?

If you switch it on. Automatic assignment offers each new order to the least-loaded driver whose zone covers the pickup point, relative to their capacity for the day. The driver accepts or declines within the time limit you set; with no answer, the job moves on to the next driver. Whatever the system cannot place — zone not covered, nobody available, capacity reached, declined by everyone — goes to the dispatcher with the reason, and manual assignment is always available.

How do you locate a customer without a precise address?

Paste the location link the customer shared — Google Maps, WhatsApp, Waze, Apple Maps or OpenStreetMap — or drop a pin on the map. The original link is forwarded to the driver, who opens the exact spot in their navigation app with one tap.

Is the software available in Arabic?

Yes. The interfaces are in French and Arabic, with a fully mirrored right-to-left layout in Arabic.

Is our data kept separate from other carriers?

Yes. Each company has its own data space, isolated from the others. Roles limit what each person can see and do, and sensitive actions are logged.

How do our merchants create their orders?

From their portal, in a minute: they can even paste the location link their customer sent, then follow every order in real time.

Can we run Portrail under our own brand?

Yes, as a white-label product: your merchants and customers work with your name.

How much does Portrail cost?

Pricing depends on the number of couriers: Starter at MAD 390 per month up to 5 couriers, Pro at MAD 990 for 6 to 20 couriers, Business at MAD 2,490 for 21 to 60 couriers (excl. tax), after a 30-day free trial. Beyond that, a custom plan.
